Diamond and Pencil Lead Are the Same Atom. Here's the Difference.

The hardest natural material and the tip of a pencil are made of exactly the same atom: carbon. The difference is how the atoms hold on to each other: four bonds in a rigid 3D frame, or three bonds in sheets that slide.
