Why It Is Harder to Reach the Sun Than to Leave the Solar System

Reaching the Sun is harder than leaving the solar system. Everything launched from Earth already moves sideways at about 30 km/s, and you have to cancel almost all of it. The Parker Solar Probe used Venus as a brake.
