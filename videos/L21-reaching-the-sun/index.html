Why Is the Sun So Hard to Reach? Orbits and Gravity Assists Explained

The Sun holds almost all the mass in the solar system, yet it is the hardest place to reach: harder than leaving the solar system. This deep dive explains why an orbit is falling and missing, why reaching the Sun takes more than twice the change in speed of escaping it, how gravity assists work from the planet's view and the Sun's, and how the Parker Solar Probe used Venus seven times to reach 192 km/s.
