Why a 10 mm Pin Won't Go in a 10 mm Hole (Fits and H7/g6 Explained)

A 10.00 mm pin does not go into a 10.00 mm hole. A fit is a deliberate difference of a few microns between the two: leave a gap and the pin slides, overlap them and the joint never comes apart. This video shows clearance, transition and interference fits, what H7/g6 means, and how a shrink fit carries torque with no key at all.

In this video

  • Why equal sizes jam
  • Clearance, transition and interference fits
  • Reading H7/g6
  • Shrink fits: heat the hub, cool the shaft
  • 79 MPa of grip and 2,300 N·m with no key
