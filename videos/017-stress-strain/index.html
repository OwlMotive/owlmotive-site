Stress vs Strain: Why Steel Bends Before It Breaks

Pull a steel bar and plot force against stretch. The curve has a straight part, a yield point, a long plastic region and a final neck and break. Every number a designer uses comes from it: modulus, yield strength, ultimate strength, ductility.

The video runs the test on steel and on a brittle material, and shows what each feature of the curve means for a real part.

In this video

  • Elastic region and Young's modulus
  • Yield strength and the 0.2 % offset
  • Plastic deformation, necking and fracture
  • Ductile versus brittle materials
