Rust Is a Battery: Why Iron Eats Itself

Rust is not iron slowly going bad. It is a battery: one drop of water on bare steel builds a working electric cell, with the steel as the fuel. This video follows the electrons and the ions around the loop, shows why rust never seals the surface, why it cracks concrete from the inside, and why a zinc coating stops it.
