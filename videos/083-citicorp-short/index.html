One Storm Away: The Citicorp Tower in 100 Seconds

A 59-storey tower in Manhattan stands on four columns at the middle of its sides, not its corners. In 1978 a student asked what happens when the wind hits it diagonally. The answer: bolted joints that a storm expected once every sixteen years could break. It was repaired in secret, at night.

In this video

  • Columns at mid-face, because of a church
  • Diagonal wind: 40 % more force in some braces
  • Welded joints built as bolted ones
  • Once in 16 years, then once in 700
