Hydrogen Embrittlement: Why a Good Bolt Snaps a Day After Tightening

A bolt of the right grade, tightened to the right torque, snaps a day later with no extra load. The cause went in at the factory: plating released hydrogen, the smallest atom there is, into the steel. This video shows where the hydrogen goes, why it gathers at the first thread, why only the strongest bolts are at risk, and how baking drives it out.
