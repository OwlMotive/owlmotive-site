Why Bolts Really Loosen (and Why Spring Washers Don't Help)

A bolt tightened to the right torque is loose a few weeks later, and everybody blames vibration. Vibration along the bolt does almost nothing. Sideways slip does all the damage: every slip lets the thread unwind a little. On the Junker test a spring washer loses its preload as fast as a plain nut.

  • Shaking along the axis: nothing happens
  • The Junker test: sideways slip
  • Why spring washers fail it
  • What works: preload, wedge-locking washers, thread adhesive
