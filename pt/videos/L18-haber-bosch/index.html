Haber–Bosch Explained: How We Pull Fertilizer Out of Thin Air

About half the nitrogen atoms in your body came out of a steel reactor. This deep dive explains the Haber-Bosch process: why the triple bond of nitrogen is so hard to break, how pressure, temperature and an iron catalyst are balanced against each other, why only 15 % reacts per pass and the loop that fixes that, where the hydrogen comes from, and what feeding half of humanity this way costs.
