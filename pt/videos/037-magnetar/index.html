Magnetars: The Strongest Magnets in the Universe

A magnetar is a dead star twenty kilometres across with a magnetic field of a hundred billion tesla, strong enough to kill from a thousand kilometres away. This video puts it on a chart beside every magnet humans have built, shows where the field comes from, and what happened when one of them flared in 2004.
