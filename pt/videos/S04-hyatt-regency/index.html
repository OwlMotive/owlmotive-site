One Changed Detail Doubled the Load: The Hyatt Regency Walkway Collapse (1981)

On 17 July 1981, two walkways fell into the crowded atrium of the Hyatt Regency in Kansas City. The cause was a change to one connection detail: one long hanger rod became two shorter rods, and the nut under the fourth-floor walkway ended up carrying both walkways. This Case File follows the load path, the box beam that let the nut pull through, the laboratory tests, and how the change got past every check.
