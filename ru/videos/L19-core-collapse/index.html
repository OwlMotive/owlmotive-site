Supernova Explained: How a Giant Star's Core Collapses in One Second

On 23 February 1987, three detectors underground caught 25 neutrinos from an exploding star, three hours before its light. This deep dive follows a giant star to its end: the fuel stages, the iron core, the collapse in less than a second, and the neutron star left behind.
