Where Gold Comes From: The Neutron Star Collision of 2017 Explained

On 17 August 2017, detectors on Earth felt a ripple in space pass through them, and 1.7 seconds later a satellite caught a flash of gamma rays from the same patch of sky. Two neutron stars had collided 130 million light-years away. This deep dive explains why fusion cannot make gold, how neutron stars spiral together, what the waves and the light told us, and how the glowing debris settled where gold comes from.
