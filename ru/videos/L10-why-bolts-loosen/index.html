Why Bolts Loosen (and Why Spring Washers Don't Stop It): The Junker Test Explained

A bolt tightened to the right torque is loose a few weeks later, and everybody blames vibration. Vibration alone does almost nothing. One particular motion does all the damage: sideways slip between the clamped parts. This is the long version: the Junker test, why each slip lets the thread unwind a little, and then every locking method in turn, with what the test says about each.

Every number on screen is computed for one M12 joint at 40 kN of preload.

В этом видео

  • Every tight bolt is trying to unwind: 11 N·m of 96
  • Shaking along the axis does nothing
  • The Junker test and the slip limit of 6 kN
  • Preload first, then take the shear elsewhere
  • Spring washers, nylon-insert nuts, thread adhesive, wedge-locking washers, wire and pins, double nuts
  • Loosening without turning: embedding and temperature
