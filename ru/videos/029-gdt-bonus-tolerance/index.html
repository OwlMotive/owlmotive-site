Position Tolerance and MMC: Why a Bigger Hole Earns a Bigger Tolerance

A plate is rejected because one hole is out of position. It would have bolted on without trouble. A plus-or-minus tolerance makes a square zone and ignores the size of the hole. Position tolerance with the MMC modifier makes a round zone that grows as the hole gets bigger, and accepts every part that actually fits.
