The Spacecraft Lost to a Unit Conversion: Mars Climate Orbiter (1999)

On 23 September 1999, the Mars Climate Orbiter reached Mars after nine and a half months and was never heard from again. It flew 169 km too low because one team's software reported thruster pushes in pound-force seconds while the navigators' model expected newton seconds. This Case File follows the one-sided solar array, the small pushes it caused, the file nobody checked, and the warning signs before arrival.
